Make your work easier to discover.

This page is a starting point for building your online presence and sharing what you do.

Two platforms, one fuller story

LinkedIn can help people learn about your experience, interests, and goals. GitHub lets them explore the projects and code behind your work. Together, they give people a clearer picture of what you do and what you are learning.

Keep it yours

Start with a clear introduction and a project you are proud of. Update your profiles as your skills and interests grow, and connect with people whose work inspires you.
